❓ FAQ

Common Questions, Straight Answers

Everything you want to know about studying abroad with Shreya Namankan.

No matching questions found — ask us directly.

Is the consultancy service really free for students?
Yes — 100% free. Shreya Namankan charges absolutely nothing to students. We are compensated by our university partners, so every service — from profile evaluation to visa preparation — is provided at zero cost to you.
Which countries can I apply to?
We support applications to 15+ countries including Germany, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, USA, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Poland, Ireland, Singapore, South Korea, Japan, Sweden, Switzerland, Denmark, Finland, Belgium, Austria, Czech Republic, Malaysia, and UAE.
Is IELTS mandatory for all universities?
Not always. Many of our partner universities offer IELTS waivers for students who have studied in English-medium institutions. We assess your eligibility and apply for the waiver on your behalf — saving you time and the IELTS exam cost.
Is it true that public universities in Germany are free?
Yes! Most public universities in Germany charge only a semester fee (€150–€350) for administration and transport. There is no tuition fee for international students at most state universities. This makes Germany one of the most affordable study destinations in the world.
How do I get started?
Simply fill out the contact form on this page or send us a WhatsApp message at +91-9135288718. We will schedule a free profile evaluation call and guide you from there. No commitment required.
Can you help me get an education loan?
Yes. We help you identify scholarship opportunities and connect you with trusted banks and NBFCs that offer student education loans — including collateral-free options for eligible profiles. We guide you through the documentation process at no extra charge.
Is it safe to study abroad as an Indian student?
Studying abroad is generally very safe. Our students are part of a community group where they receive pre-arrival city guides, safety tips, and ongoing support. We connect you with existing students in your destination city before you even land.
What is the minimum eligibility to apply?
Requirements vary by country and program. In general, you need a 10+2 or bachelor's degree (depending on the program level), a basic understanding of English, and a valid passport. Contact us for a free profile check — there is no minimum requirement to start the conversation.
